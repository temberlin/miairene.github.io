FUMAGE

Holding a piece of paper above a candle flame at an angle to capture the soot, I created these wispy, flame-like shapes that, with the colors inverted and altered, created a stylized fire used throughout the video. I like the idea of reality and its representation having an indirect but indexical relationship; the poetry of making digital flames out of physical soot.
